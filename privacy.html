Privacy

The short version

Social Scrub keeps your checkmarks in your own browser. There's no account, no tracking and no server that stores your data. We never see which platforms you use.

What gets saved

Which platforms you checked, whether you're keeping or deleting each one, the steps you've done, and the names of any platforms you added yourself. It's saved in this browser's local storage, on this device only, and stays there until you remove it.

To remove it, use "Clear all data" at the bottom of the sheet, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. On a shared or work computer, a private window keeps nothing once you close it.

Downloads

The Excel and CSV files are made on your device. Nothing is uploaded to create them. Once downloaded, they're files like any other, so keep them somewhere safe.

No analytics, no third parties

Social Scrub doesn't use analytics or cookies. The page loads nothing from other sites: the fonts, logos and code all come from this site. Links to other platforms only open when you click them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. As with any website, your IP address is visible to the host when the page loads. See Vercel's privacy policy for how they handle it.

Questions

The code is open. If something here is unclear, or you spot a problem, open an issue on GitHub.
