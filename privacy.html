Privacy

The short version

Social Scrub keeps your checklist in your own browser. There's no account and no server that stores your data. We never see which platforms you have.

What gets saved

Which platforms you checked, whether you're keeping or deleting each one, the steps you've done, the names of any platforms you added yourself, and the light or dark theme if you picked one. It's saved in this browser's local storage, on this device only, and stays there until you remove it.

To remove it, use "Clear all data" at the bottom of the sheet, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. On a shared or work computer, a private window keeps nothing once you close it.

Downloads

The Excel and CSV files are made on your device. Nothing is uploaded to create them. Once downloaded, they're files like any other, so keep them somewhere safe.

What we count

When you open a page, we count the visit: which page, which site linked you here (just its address, not the rest of the link), and your browser's language, screen size and type of device. We also count actions, like "a box was checked" or "a file was downloaded", with at most the priority group or column. Counts never say which platform and never include anything you typed. There are no cookies.

Counts go to our own server first. It scrambles your IP address, keeping only enough to tell your rough region, and then passes the count to our analytics (a copy of Umami we run ourselves). The key used to scramble addresses is kept only in memory and replaced every day, so afterwards nobody, including us, can match a count to an address.

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted.

Nothing from other sites

The page loads nothing from other sites: the fonts, logos and code all come from this site. Links to other platforms only open when you click them.

Hosting

The site runs on Railway. As with any website, the host can see your IP address when the page loads. See Railway's privacy policy for how they handle it.

Questions

The code is open. If something here is unclear, or you spot a problem, open an issue on GitHub.
